How Solar Check calculates

1. Your LGA supplies latitude/longitude. 2. Current weather/solar radiation is requested for that coordinate. 3. Long-term solar resource is estimated from NASA POWER climatology. 4. Panel capacity and a configurable performance ratio convert solar resource into electricity. 5. Orientation/tilt are applied as a practical adjustment when the selected setup differs from the local optimum.

Accuracy note: This is an engineering estimate, not a guaranteed meter reading. Actual production can differ because of clouds, shading, dust, panel temperature, panel/inverter quality, wiring, battery charging losses and system downtime. For bankable design work, use a site survey and detailed PV simulation.

Data & privacy

Administrative locations: Nigeria administrative-division data containing 37 top-level divisions and 774 LGAs. Current weather: Open-Meteo. Long-term solar climate: NASA POWER. Solar resource methodology is aligned with the concept of PV energy yield used by the Global Solar Atlas, which publishes location-based PVOUT in kWh/kWp.

No account is required. The page does not send your form values to Solar Check's own server.
